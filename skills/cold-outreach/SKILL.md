# Cold Outreach That Isn't a Robot

Write a cold email a busy person would actually reply to. This only works when there is a true, specific reason for the outreach, so ask for it before writing.

## Inputs you need

- The recipient's title and company.
- **The real, specific thing you noticed about them** (a launch, a hire, a post, a job opening, a change). This is the whole email. If there is nothing true and specific, say so and stop; do not manufacture fake familiarity.
- The one outcome you help with, in plain words.

## Rules for the email

- Under 90 words.
- Open with them, not with you or your company.
- One specific observation. One low-friction question, not a meeting demand.
- Banned: "hope this finds you well," "quick call," "circle back," "touch base," buzzwords, and any fake "I loved your post" familiarity.
- No em dashes.

## Output

Two versions, each with its own short subject line (under 6 words, lowercase-optional, no clickbait). Note in one line what is different about each so the sender can pick.
